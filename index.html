How I work

I've worked across HR. I know how the pieces fit together.

From talent development and business partnering to operations, systems and global transformation, I've seen how decisions in one area affect another. That perspective helps me ask better questions, challenge assumptions and find solutions that work for the business and its people.

I think in systems, not silos.

I understand how people, processes, systems and decisions connect, and how a change in one place shows up somewhere else. That helps me see the bigger picture, anticipate the knock-on effects and design solutions that work in practice.

Every organisation is different.

I stay on top of HR trends and new thinking, and that matters. But the real question is what works for your organisation and its context. I listen, ask questions and take time to understand the business, its people and its culture before deciding what will actually work.

Structure should make work easier, not harder.

Good processes and operating models need to make sense to the people using them. I focus on practical solutions, clear accountability and bringing people along so that changes work in everyday practice.

Get the foundations right before adding AI.

Clean data, clear processes and good governance come first. Without them, AI simply adds complexity. I see AI as an opportunity to make work easier and improve decisions, while keeping people and good judgement at the centre.

HRIS & People data

Turning fragmented People data into reliable business intelligence

Across 11 countries, we transformed D365 into a single source of People data, bringing all workers into the system and consolidating remuneration, contracts and contractor information previously held in spreadsheets.

We replaced a complex legacy project structure with a functional hierarchy, reducing organisational units by 70%, while standardising definitions, naming conventions and approval workflows.

The result was a simpler, more reliable HR data foundation, stronger governance across HR, IT and Finance, and BI reporting that gave leaders better visibility of their workforce and the information they needed for budgeting and workforce planning.

Practical AI in HR

An 'Ask HR' proof of concept agent

Recognising a gap in accessible HR information, we first built two People & Culture intranet sites: one for all employees, providing a central source of HR information, and another for the international HR team to share knowledge, improve collaboration and work more consistently across countries.

These sites created the knowledge foundation for an 'Ask HR' AI agent proof of concept, where we explored the data, processes, governance and cost model needed to provide reliable, scalable employee support.

AI in learning

Using AI to build technical learning for sales teams

I created a Technical Fundamentals learning journey in Eloomi to onboard new frontline salespeople. I used AI to develop the learning content, working closely with the company's technical experts to validate its accuracy, review the learner experience and ensure the overall programme met the learning objectives.
